# Phu Quoc: Two 4-Day Plans

Two versions of the same trip. **Plan A** is for a resort in the **north**. **Plan B** is for a resort in the **south**.

**Both plans include all five of your must-see places:**

1. Vinpearl Safari
2. VinWonders
3. Hon Thom cable car
4. Grand World
5. Phu Quoc Night Market

## What you told me

- **4 days** in Phu Quoc.
- **Days 1 and 2:** sightseeing.
- **Days 3 and 4:** resort days, completely chilling. You can go out for **2 hours at most** on each of those days.
- **Travelling with young children.**
- **Getting around by taxi or Grab.**
- The five places above must be in both plans.

Details for every place (ratings, review analysis, photos, credits) are in the [final guide](phu-quoc-final-guide.md). The [interactive map](https://claude.ai/artifact/3WgF73jGbT7Gk4SqWVNuWi) shows where they are (private until the owner shares it).

## How the five places fit the island

The five places sit in three separate areas:

| Area | Places | How long to spend |
|---|---|---|
| **North-west** (close together, 10–20 min apart†) | Vinpearl Safari, VinWonders, Grand World | Safari about 3 hours. VinWonders 4–6 hours†. Grand World 2–3 hours† |
| **South** (An Thoi) | Hon Thom cable car | The whole Sun World Hon Thom visit is 6–8 hours. A half day is about 4 hours |
| **West** (Duong Dong town) | Phu Quoc Night Market | 1–2 hours, in the evening |

So each plan has **two sightseeing days**:

- **A north-west day:** Safari, VinWonders and Grand World.
- **A south-and-west day:** cable car in the morning, night market in the evening.

The only thing that changes between the plans is which day is close to your resort and which day is the long taxi day.

## How I built the plans

- **Midday rest.** It is 30–33 °C, and about 35 °C at midday. Each long day has a rest break.
- **Phu Quoc Prison is left out.** Reviews and blogs call it graphic. It is not for young children.
- **Times marked †** are my own estimates, mostly drive times from map distances. Times without † come from the sources in the final guide. Opening hours and prices differ between sources, so check them.
- **Taxi tip.** At remote spots like the An Thoi cable car station, taxis can be hard to find for the trip back. I have not verified how easy it is, so ask your resort to book the return or ask the driver to wait.
- **Rain swap.** If a sightseeing day is rained out, swap it with a resort day. Sea and outdoor plans are less predictable from May to October, and mid-October is a transition month.
- **If you land after midday on Day 1,** do only the evening part of Day 1 and push the rest back a day. The resort days are your buffer.

## The two plans at a glance

| | **Plan A: resort in the north** | **Plan B: resort in the south** |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | **North-west day, next to your resort:** Safari, rest, VinWonders, Grand World | **South day, next to your resort:** cable car and Hon Thom, rest, then Dinh Cau and the night market in Duong Dong |
| **Day 2** | **Long taxi day:** cable car and Hon Thom, then Dinh Cau and the night market in Duong Dong on the way home | **Long taxi day:** Safari, VinWonders, Grand World |
| **Day 3** | Resort. Optional 2 hours: Starfish Beach at low tide | Resort. Optional 2 hours: Khem Beach in the morning |
| **Day 4** | Resort and departure | Resort and departure. Optional 1 hour: Sao Beach early |
| **Longest day** | Day 2, about 13 hours door to door | Day 2, about 14 hours door to door |

# Plan A: Resort in the north

**Where this is:** The north-west coast around Bai Dai and Ganh Dau, where the big Vin parks are. One reviewer said the Vinpearl resort is in the north, with many activities in the south about an hour away.

**Drive times from a north resort†:** Safari, VinWonders and Grand World 10–20 min. Duong Dong 30–40 min. An Thoi cable car station 60–75 min. Starfish Beach 20–30 min. Airport 30–40 min.

## Day 1: North-west day (Safari, VinWonders, Grand World)

Everything is close to your resort, so you can rest in the middle of the day. That makes it easier for small children.

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30 | Taxi to **Vinpearl Safari** | 10–20 min†. The first safari bus leaves at 09:00. The safari zone stops admitting visitors after about 15:00. |
| 09:00–12:00 | **Vinpearl Safari** (about 3 hours) | Giraffe feeding is the star. A 2026 review says kids especially enjoy it. Google 4.6 (26,636 reviews). Ticket 850,000 VND for guests 1.4 m and taller. |
| 12:00–14:30 | Lunch, then rest at the resort | Pool and nap. This is what makes the day work. |
| 14:30 | Taxi to **VinWonders** | 10–20 min†. |
| 15:00–18:45 | **VinWonders** (3.5–4 hours here, a shorter version of the 4–6 hour full visit†) | Water park, rides and aquarium. Bring swimsuits. Indoor areas give relief from the heat. Google 4.8 (17,816 reviews). Ticket 950,000 VND for guests 1.4 m and taller. A combo with the Safari is about 1,500,000 VND. |
| 19:00 | Taxi to **Grand World** | 10–20 min†. |
| 19:15–21:00 | **Grand World** (about 2 hours) | Dinner, the canals ("Little Venice"), cafes and nightly shows. It looks best after sunset. Google 4.4 (12,620 reviews). The Grand World night market is listed 17:00 to 02:00. |
| 21:00 | Taxi back to the resort | Kids in bed by about 21:45. |

- **Book ahead:** The combo ticket on the official site or a reseller. Compare prices.
- **Reviews say:** VinWonders reviewers found short queues, good food and a strong water park. Some mention ride closures and strict water-park staff. I could not capture Grand World review text.
- **Lighter option:** Skip Grand World on Day 1. Do it on Day 3 as your 2-hour resort-day outing, since it is only 10–20 minutes† from the resort.

## Day 2: Cable car in the morning, night market in the evening (long taxi day)

You cross the island once. The night market sits on the way back, in Duong Dong.

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | Taxi to the **An Thoi cable car station** | 60–75 min†. A free shuttle runs to the station. Check the timetable at the gate. |
| 09:00 | **Hon Thom cable car** out | Over the sea and the An Thoi islands. About 8 km of cable, around 150 m up. Google 4.5 (19,408 reviews). |
| 09:30–13:30 | **Sun World Hon Thom Nature Park** (about 4 hours) | Games and rides. Exotica Park opens about 09:30 in one listing. Have lunch on the island. |
| 13:45 | **Cable car back** | One source gives the windows as about 09:00–11:30 and 13:30–17:00, with a midday break. The return can be crowded. |
| 14:30 | Taxi to Duong Dong | 40–50 min†. Ask the resort to book the pick-up at the station. |
| 15:15–17:15 | Rest (a cafe or hotel lobby with air conditioning) | The kids can nap in the taxi too. |
| 17:30–18:00 | **Dinh Cau** (20–30 min†) | Small sea-goddess shrine with a blue-and-white lighthouse. Google 4.5 (3,675 reviews). Sunset is about 17:30–18:00†. |
| 18:00–19:30 | **Phu Quoc Night Market** (1–2 hours) | Street food and seafood. Eat dinner here. One guide suggests 18:30–21:00 as the best window. Google 4.0 (6,575 reviews). |
| 19:45 | Taxi back to the north resort | 30–40 min†. Arrive about 20:30. |

- **Total:** about 13 hours door to door, with about 4 hours on Hon Thom and 1.5 hours at the market.
- **Watch out for:** The cable car has a **midday break**, and the return trip can be crowded. At the night market, agree prices first, eat cooked seafood unless you know what you are buying, and watch for bait-and-switch sales. Do not go to Hon Thom for the beach. The "Pineapple beach" listing there scores only 2.9 (51 reviews).
- **More water fun:** Aquatopia water park is included in a round-trip cable car ticket, but it needs 2–3 more hours† and makes the day even longer. Skip it unless the kids ask.
- **Lighter option:** Take the cable car and 3 hours on Hon Thom, then go straight home and skip the night market. This drops one of your five, so use it only if the kids are done.

## Day 3: Resort day

Fully relaxed. Optional outing of up to **2 hours**:

- **Starfish Beach (Rach Vem)** at **low tide** (when the sea is at its lowest). 20–30 min† by taxi. Google 3.8 (3,636 reviews). The water is shallow and not for swimming. **Look at the starfish but do not lift them out of the water.** The road is bumpy, and some reviewers call it a tourist trap with high prices. Skip it if the kids are tired.

## Day 4: Resort day and departure

Fully relaxed. The airport is about 30–40 minutes† from a north resort. Leave extra time.

# Plan B: Resort in the south

**Where this is:** The south coast around An Thoi, Khem Beach or Sao Beach. The cable car station is in An Thoi.

**Drive times from a south resort†:** Cable car station 15–25 min. Khem Beach 10–20 min. Sao Beach 10–20 min. Duong Dong 40–50 min. Safari 60–75 min. VinWonders and Grand World about 60–75 min. Airport 35–45 min.

## Day 1: South day (cable car, then the night market in the evening)

The cable car is close to your resort, so you can rest at the resort in the afternoon before the evening in town.

| 09:30–13:30 | **Sun World Hon Thom Nature Park** (about 4 hours) | Games and rides. Exotica Park opens about 09:30 in one listing. Have lunch on the island. |
| 13:45 | **Cable car back** | One source gives the windows as about 09:00–11:30 and 13:30–17:00, with a midday break. The return can be crowded. |
| 14:30 | Taxi to the resort | Ask the resort to book the pick-up at the station. |
| 15:00–17:00 | Lunch top-up, pool and nap | |
| 17:00 | Taxi to Duong Dong | 40–50 min†. |
| 17:45–18:15 | **Dinh Cau** (20–30 min†) | Small sea-goddess shrine. Google 4.5 (3,675 reviews). Sunset is about 17:30–18:00†. |

- **More water fun:** Aquatopia is included in a round-trip cable car ticket. If you want it, spend a full Sun World day (6–8 hours) and skip the evening in town. This drops one of your five, so decide on the day.

## Day 2: North-west day (Safari, VinWonders, Grand World), the long taxi day

This is the hardest day of both plans. Everything is in one area, but it is about an hour each way from your resort. Let the kids nap in the taxi.

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | Taxi to **Vinpearl Safari** | 60–75 min†. Arrive by 09:00. The first safari bus leaves at 09:00. |
| 09:00–12:00 | **Vinpearl Safari** (about 3 hours) | Giraffe feeding. Google 4.6 (26,636 reviews). Ticket 850,000 VND for guests 1.4 m and taller. |
| 12:00–13:00 | Lunch | |
| 13:00 | Taxi to **VinWonders** | 10–20 min†. |
| 13:30–17:30 | **VinWonders** (about 4 hours) | Water park, rides and aquarium. Bring swimsuits. Indoor areas give relief from the heat. Google 4.8 (17,816 reviews). Ticket 950,000 VND for guests 1.4 m and taller. A combo with the Safari is about 1,500,000 VND. |
| 17:45 | Taxi to **Grand World** | 10–20 min†. |
| 18:00–20:00 | **Grand World** (about 2 hours) | Dinner, canals, cafes and shows. It looks best after sunset. Google 4.4 (12,620 reviews). |
| 20:15 | Taxi back to the south resort | 60–75 min†. Arrive about 21:30. |

- **Total:** about 14 hours door to door. It is a heavy day for young children.
- **Ways to make it easier:**
  - Eat an early dinner at VinWonders, cut Grand World to about 1 hour, and head home by 19:00.
  - Shorten VinWonders to 3 hours.
  - Book a private driver for the day so you do not hunt for taxis between parks.
  - If it is still too much, spend one night at a north resort on Day 2 and move your resort days. That changes where you stay, so only do it if you want to.

## Day 3: Resort day

Fully relaxed. Optional outing of up to **2 hours**:

- **Khem Beach** in the morning. 10–20 min† by taxi. Google 4.4 (2,109 reviews). One guide calls it "probably the quietest" beach on the island, and a Sao Beach reviewer switched to it because it was cleaner.

## Day 4: Resort day and departure

Fully relaxed. Nothing outside is needed. Optional, 1 hour at most:

- **Sao Beach** early in the morning. Google 3.9 (8,202 reviews). White sand and clear water, but litter is the top complaint, and sun beds cost about 100,000–150,000 VND. Bring your own towel.
- **Leaving:** The airport is about 35–45 minutes† from a south resort.

## Which plan is easier?

- **Plan A** has a gentle Day 1 right next to the resort, with a 2.5-hour rest, and one long day (Day 2, about 13 hours).
- **Plan B** has a gentle Day 1 close to the resort, and one very long day (Day 2, about 14 hours, with 2 hours of driving each way).
- **Why Day 1 is the near-resort day in both:** If you arrive in the morning or are jet-lagged, a short transfer on Day 1 is easier. The long taxi day comes second, after you have settled in.
- **If you want the shortest driving overall,** Plan A is a little easier, because the three north-west places are next to the resort and the only long drive is the cable car.

## Things to know for both plans

1. **Season.** Dry season is roughly November to April. Wet season is roughly May to October. In the wet season expect some rain and a rougher sea.
2. **Heat.** Plan outdoor sights before 11:00 or after 15:00. Bring hats, sun cream and water.
3. **Child height and tickets.** The prices I found are for guests 1.4 m and taller. Smaller children have different ticket rules, so check the official Vinpearl and Sun World pages.
4. **Opening hours conflict between sources.** The Safari opens about 08:30 or 09:00. VinWonders is listed from 09:00 or 10:00 to 19:30. The cable car has a midday break. Confirm everything the day before.
5. **Money and scams.** Agree taxi fares and sun-bed prices before you start. Watch for bait-and-switch sales at the night market.
6. **Visa.** Guides say visitors can stay up to 30 days visa-free on Phu Quoc if they fly directly to the island. This covers the island only, and the guides are not government sources. Confirm with official Vietnamese pages. See the [Vietnam tourism visa explainer](https://www.vietnamtourism.com/en/phu-quoc-visa-free-entry-how-the-30-day-exemption-works-and-who-qualifies).
7. **Sunset time and exact drive times are estimates.** Check your taxi app on the day.
