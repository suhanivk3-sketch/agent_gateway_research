# Phu Quoc: Two 4-Day Plans

Two versions of the same trip. **Plan A** is for a resort in the **north**. **Plan B** is for a resort in the **south**.

## What you told me

- **4 days** in Phu Quoc.
- **Days 1 and 2:** sightseeing.
- **Days 3 and 4:** resort days, completely chilling. You can go out for **2 hours at most** on each of those days.
- **Travelling with young children.**
- **Getting around by taxi or Grab.**
- You want to **experience Phu Quoc**, so each plan mixes theme parks, sea views, sunsets and local life.

Every attraction here comes from the [final guide](phu-quoc-final-guide.md). That file has ratings, review analysis, photos and credits for each place. The [interactive map](https://claude.ai/artifact/3WgF73jGbT7Gk4SqWVNuWi) shows where they all are (private until the owner shares it).

## How I built the plans

- **One area per day.** The north and south ends of the island are about an hour apart, which is long for small children in a taxi.
- **Midday rest.** It is 30–33 °C, and about 35 °C at midday. Each long day has a break at the resort or in the shade.
- **Phu Quoc Prison is left out.** Reviews and blogs call it graphic (reconstructed cells and "tiger cages"). It is not for young children.
- **Times marked †** are my own estimates, mostly drive times from map distances. Times without † come from sources in the final guide. Check opening hours and prices before you go.
- **Taxi tip.** At remote spots (the Hon Thom station, Rach Vem), taxis can be hard to find for the trip back. I have not verified how easy it is, so ask your resort to book the return trip or ask the driver to wait.
- **Rain swap.** If a sightseeing day is rained out, swap it with a resort day. Sea and boat plans are less predictable from May to October, and mid-October is a transition month.
- **If you land after midday on Day 1,** do only the evening part of Day 1 and push the rest back a day. The resort days are your buffer.

|---|---|---|

# Plan A: Resort in the north

**Where this is:** The north-west coast around Bai Dai and Ganh Dau, where the big Vin parks are. One reviewer said the Vinpearl resort is in the north, with many activities in the south about an hour away.

**Drive times from a north resort†:** Safari, VinWonders and Grand World 10–20 min. Duong Dong 30–40 min. Starfish Beach 20–30 min. Airport 30–40 min.

## Day 1: Theme-park day, right next door

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30 | Taxi to **Vinpearl Safari** | 10–20 min†. The first safari bus leaves at 09:00, and the safari zone stops admitting visitors after about 15:00. |
| 09:00–12:00 | **Vinpearl Safari** (about 3 hours) | Giraffe feeding is the star. A 2026 review says kids especially enjoy it. Ticket 850,000 VND for guests 1.4 m and taller. Check child prices. |
| 12:00–15:00 | Lunch, then rest at the resort | Pool and nap. This is what makes the day work for small children. |
| 15:00 | Taxi to **VinWonders** | 10–20 min†. |
| 15:30–19:00 | **VinWonders** (4–6 hours† is a full visit, so this is a shorter version) | Water park, rides and aquarium. Bring swimsuits. Indoor areas give relief from the heat. Ticket 950,000 VND for guests 1.4 m and taller. A combo with the Safari is about 1,500,000 VND. |
| 19:00 | Dinner, then back to the resort | |

- **Book ahead:** The combo ticket on the official site or a reseller. Compare prices, since resellers can differ.
- **Reviews say:** VinWonders reviewers found short queues, good food and a strong water park. Some mention ride closures and strict water-park staff.
- **Too much?** Move VinWonders to Day 3 or 4 and use Day 1 only for the Safari.

## Day 2: Duong Dong in the evening

Spend the morning at the resort. Go out in the afternoon so the kids can swim first and see the town at its best.

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 15:00 | Taxi to Duong Dong | 30–40 min†. |
| 15:45–16:45 | **Ngoc Hien Pearl Farm** (30–60 min†) | A free guided tour is offered, according to one guide. Expect a sales pitch at the shop. Google rating 4.2 (3,754 reviews). |
| 17:00–18:30 | **Sunset Sanato** (1.5–2 hours†) | Beach club for the sunset. Sunset is about 17:30–18:00†. Google rating 4.0 (2,666 reviews). Check prices before you sit down. |
| 18:45–19:15 | **Dinh Cau** (20–30 min†) | Small sea-goddess shrine with a striped lighthouse. Google rating 4.5 (3,675 reviews). |
| 19:15–20:45 | **Phu Quoc Night Market** (1–2 hours) | Street food and seafood. Eat an early dinner here. One guide suggests 18:30–21:00 as the best window. |
| 21:00 | Taxi back | Aim to leave by 21:00 so the kids are in bed by 22:00. |

- **Watch out for:** Night-market prices and scams. Agree prices first. Eat cooked seafood unless you know what you are buying.
- **Reviews say:** Dinh Cau is short and easy. The market is lively but touristy and pricey (Google 4.0).
- **Tip:** Hail the return taxi a short walk from the market entrance, not at the gate.

## Day 3: Resort day

Fully relaxed. Optional outing of up to **2 hours**:

- **Grand World after dark** (2 hours at most). Canals, cafes, shops and nightly shows, about 10–20 minutes† from a north resort. Google rating 4.4 (12,620 reviews). Go after sunset, and be back by about 21:00.

## Day 4: Resort day and departure

Fully relaxed. Optional outing of up to **2 hours**:

- **Starfish Beach (Rach Vem)** at **low tide** (when the sea is at its lowest). 20–30 min† by taxi. Google rating 3.8 (3,636 reviews). The water is shallow and not for swimming. **Look at the starfish but do not lift them out of the water.** The road is bumpy and some reviewers call it a tourist trap with high prices. Skip it if the kids are tired.
- **Leaving:** The airport is about 30–40 minutes† from a north resort. Leave extra time.

# Plan B: Resort in the south

**Where this is:** The south coast around An Thoi, Khem Beach or Sao Beach. The cable car station is in An Thoi.

**Drive times from a south resort†:** Cable car station 15–25 min. Khem Beach 10–20 min. Sao Beach 10–20 min. Duong Dong 40–50 min. Safari 60–75 min. Airport 35–45 min.

## Day 1: Cable car and Sun World Hon Thom (full day, close to the resort)

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30 | Taxi to the **An Thoi cable car station** | 15–25 min†. A free shuttle runs to the station. Check the timetable posted at the gate. |
| 09:00 | **Cable car** to Hon Thom island | Over the sea and the An Thoi islands, about 8 km of cable and 150 m up. Google rating 4.5 (19,408 reviews). |
| 09:30–11:30 | **Sun World Hon Thom Nature Park** | Games and rides. One listing says Exotica Park opens at 09:30. |
| 11:30–13:00 | Lunch and shade | |
| 13:00–16:00 | **Aquatopia water park** | Slides and splash zones. One listing says it runs 10:00–16:45. A round-trip cable car ticket includes Aquatopia. |
| 16:15 | Cable car back | The afternoon window ends about 17:00 in one source, so do not cut it close. |
| 17:00 | Taxi to the resort | Ask the resort to arrange the return. |

- **Total:** about 6–8 hours on the island, plus travel. One guide calls 6–8 hours ideal.
- **Watch out for:** The cable car has a **midday break** (one source says about 11:30–13:30). The return trip can be crowded. Do not go for the beach, since "Pineapple beach" on Hon Thom scores only 2.9.
- **Too much?** Do Aquatopia only, or stay on the island for half a day (about 4 hours).

## Day 2: Safari in the morning, Duong Dong in the evening

This is a long day, so let the kids nap in the taxi.

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 07:45 | Taxi to **Vinpearl Safari** | 60–75 min†. Arrive by 09:00. |
| 09:00–12:00 | **Vinpearl Safari** (about 3 hours) | Giraffe feeding. Ticket 850,000 VND for guests 1.4 m and taller. |
| 12:00–14:00 | Lunch, then the taxi to Duong Dong | The Safari is 25–30 min from Duong Dong by taxi. |
| 14:00–16:30 | Rest at a hotel lobby, cafe or in the car. Or visit the **Ngoc Hien Pearl Farm** (30–60 min†) | A free guided tour is offered, according to one guide. |
| 17:30–18:00 | **Dinh Cau** (20–30 min†) | Sunset is about 17:30–18:00†. |
| 18:00–19:30 | **Phu Quoc Night Market** (1–2 hours) | Early dinner here. |
| 19:30 | Taxi back to the south | 40–50 min†. Arrive about 20:30. |

- **Total:** about 12 hours door to door. This is the heavy day of Plan B.
- **If the kids prefer rides:** Swap the Safari for **VinWonders**. It is a similar drive from the south.
- **Lighter option:** Skip the night market, do Dinh Cau at sunset, and eat dinner back at the resort.

## Day 3: Resort day

Fully relaxed. Optional outing of up to **2 hours**:

- **Khem Beach** in the morning. 10–20 min† by taxi. Google rating 4.4 (2,109 reviews). One guide calls it "probably the quietest" beach on the island, and a Sao Beach reviewer switched to it because it was cleaner.

## Day 4: Resort day and departure

Fully relaxed. Nothing outside is needed. Optional, 1 hour at most:

- **Sao Beach** early in the morning. Google rating 3.9 (8,202 reviews). White sand and clear water, but litter is the top complaint, and sun beds cost about 100,000–150,000 VND. Bring your own towel. Go only if you have not had enough beach.
- **Leaving:** The airport is about 35–45 minutes† from a south resort.

## Things to know for both plans

1. **Season.** Dry season is roughly November to April. Wet season is roughly May to October. In the wet season expect some rain and a rougher sea.
2. **Heat.** Plan outdoor sights before 11:00 or after 15:00. Bring hats, sun cream and water.
3. **Child height and tickets.** The prices I found are for guests 1.4 m and taller. Smaller children have different ticket rules, so check the official Vinpearl and Sun World pages.
4. **Opening hours conflict between sources.** The Safari opens about 08:30 or 09:00. VinWonders is listed from 09:00 or 10:00 to 19:30. The cable car has a midday break. Confirm everything the day before.
5. **Money and scams.** Agree taxi fares and sun-bed prices before you start. Watch for bait-and-switch sales at the night market.
6. **Visa.** Guides say visitors can stay up to 30 days visa-free on Phu Quoc if they fly directly to the island. This covers the island only, and the guides are not government sources. Confirm with official Vietnamese pages. See the [Vietnam tourism visa explainer](https://www.vietnamtourism.com/en/phu-quoc-visa-free-entry-how-the-30-day-exemption-works-and-who-qualifies).
7. **Sunset time and exact drive times are estimates.** Check your taxi app on the day.

## What I could not confirm

- Child ticket prices and height rules at each park.
- Exact cable car hours and ticket prices.
- Drive times from specific resorts. The † times come from map distances, not from a driver.
